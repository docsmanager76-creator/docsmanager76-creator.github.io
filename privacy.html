Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who uses this app

VideoFactory is used only by its owner's team, on the team's own computers, for the team's own YouTube channels. It is not a public service.

What Google data it uses

With the account owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channels and to manage those videos (title, description, thumbnail, privacy and publish time). It reads only the basic details of the connected channel (its name and ID) to make sure the upload goes to the right channel.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token is saved only on the team's own computers, so the app can upload without signing in each time. No Google user data is sold, shared with others, used for advertising, or sent to any server other than Google's.

Limited use

VideoFactory's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The account owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the saved token then stops working and can be deleted from the computers.
