VideoFactory

VideoFactory is a private tool used by one small team to publish product-review videos on its own YouTube channels (ToolPick Pro, KitchenPick Pro, AutoPick Pro, HomePick Pro and PrimeNest Picks).

It signs in with Google only to upload those videos to the team's own channels, set their title, description, thumbnail and publish time. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
